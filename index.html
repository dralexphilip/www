Questions

When do I pay?

Only when you deploy. Sign up and build free. When your app is ready, choose where it runs in the platform's Deployment tab, confirm the price, and pay monthly or annually. Each live app has its own subscription.

How does the free trial work?

New accounts get $100 of qodon Cloud credit, valid for 30 days, to deploy and test an app on a small single-box setup. We'll remind you before the credit runs out. If you don't choose a plan by then, your app is paused and your data kept for 14 days, so you can pick up where you left off.

What does BYOI mean?

Bring Your Own Infrastructure. Your app runs on servers you own, such as your AWS account or your data centre, and you pay that provider directly.

What am I paying for on the Builder plan?

Use of the online builder, deployments into your AWS (staging and production included), and safe, versioned storage of your app's design (data models, screens, workflows) in the qodon library. Your app and data run in your account, on single box or separate services, whichever you choose.

How is Builder + Hosting priced?

Pick a topology. Single box runs everything on one server and costs less. Separate services runs the web server, API and database separately so each can scale. Then size each part: instance or services, database, storage, load balancer. Each item has a clear monthly price, plus managed operations for monitoring, patching and upgrades.

What happens if my app grows?

Nothing stops working. We tell you when you're close to what you configured and suggest the change. You only pay more once you agree.

Can I move my app later?

Yes. Move between qodon Cloud and your own infrastructure at any time, with a full export of your app and data and no exit fee.

Do you charge per user?

No. Pricing is per app and the services it uses. Your whole team can use it.
