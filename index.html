Questions

When do I pay?

Only for what runs. Sign up and build free. When your app is ready, you deploy a release to an environment and see its price before you confirm. Charges come from your prepaid balance: one-time items when they happen, and running items (environments, tenants, platforms) a thirtieth of their monthly price each day. Top up whenever you like.

How does the free trial work?

New accounts start with $100 on their balance, enough to deploy and test an app on a small single-box environment. No card is needed. When the balance runs low you top it up; nothing renews automatically.

What does BYOI mean?

Bring Your Own Infrastructure. Your app runs on servers you own, such as your AWS account or your data centre, and you pay that provider directly.

What is a platform of my own?

A separate qodon platform with its own builder, App Library, users and sign-in, for keeping a business unit or a client apart. On qodon Cloud we host and run it for a monthly price, and its environments and tenants are priced as usual. Or we move a whole platform into your own AWS for a one-time price, and you run it.

How is qodon Cloud priced?

Each environment has a size. Single box runs everything on one server and costs less. Separate services runs the web server, API and database separately so each can scale. Add-ons such as a load balancer, extra database storage or a custom domain each have a clear monthly price. Tenants and release deployments are priced per item. Every price is shown before you confirm.

What happens if my app grows?

Nothing stops working. We tell you when you're close to what you configured and suggest the change. You only pay more once you agree.

Can I move my app later?

Yes. Move between qodon Cloud and your own infrastructure at any time, with a full export of your app and data and no exit fee.

Do you charge per user?

No. You pay for what runs: environments, tenants and platforms. Your whole team can use it.

Does AI write my app's code?

No. AI only helps draft your app's configuration (data models, screens, menus and workflows) from qodon's own building blocks. You see what it proposes before anything is created, everything arrives as a draft you can review and change, and your app runs on the same qodon engine as every other app. There's no generated code to maintain or debug, and you can build everything yourself without AI if you prefer.
